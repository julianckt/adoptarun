# Digital & Printable Certificate Design

Status: open  
Type: prototype  

## Question

How should the digital web certificate and downloadable physical PDF/PNG certificate be visually styled and formatted?

### Focus Areas
- Digital Certificate interactive web view (dark mode aesthetic, glowing route polyline map, stat badges for distance/pace/elevation, share button).
- Downloadable Physical Certificate layout (print-ready high-res template, official typography, "Powered by Strava" badge).
