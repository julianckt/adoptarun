# Marketing Language & Site Copy

Status: resolved  
Type: grilling  

## Agreed Copy & Language Specifications

### 1. Hero Section
- **Headline**: Single word **"RUN"** set in `Scale VF`.
- **Hero Interaction**: A continuous-line vector dog sits in the middle of "RUN". Hovering over "RUN" morphs `Scale VF` variable font weight while the dog animates, runs off screen, and is freed.
- **Subtitle**: **None** (pure athletic & artistic minimalism).

### 2. Core Slogans
- **Primary Tagline**: *"Run for your cause. Leave your mark."*
- **Action Banner Slogan**: *"Adopt the run. Complete the route. Own the impact."*

### 3. The 3-Step Caretaker Journey
- **Step 01: Match & Commit**  
  > *"Match with an animal route, and commit to a paired charity cause."*
- **Step 02: Nurture & Grow**  
  > *"Bring your animal to life with your running steps and fundraise to nurture your cause."*
- **Step 03: Forever Guardian**  
  > *"Own your success in running and in impact! Hand off your run to its next caretaker."*

### 4. Primary Call-to-Action (CTA) Labels
- **Primary Adoption CTA**: `"Run with us"`
- **Run Completion CTA**: `"Log Your Run"` *(Sub-options: `"Sync with Strava"` & `"Upload GPX File"`)*
- **Certificate CTA**: `"Claim Adoption Certificate"`
- **Hand-Off / Share CTA**: `"Pass the Torch"`

### 5. Storytelling Framework ("Why Adopt A Run?")
- **Concept Framing**: Runs (the art) and causes are *waiting to be adopted and championed*.
- **Dual Fulfillment**: Eager runners adopt a run to achieve a personal milestone *for themselves* AND create lasting social impact *for the community*.
- **Tone**: Empowering, inviting, caretaker-oriented, and purposeful.

### 6. Adoption Impact Metrics
- `__ Runs Adopted`
- `__ Kilometers Trekked`
- `__ Causes Championed`
- `__ Participants Involved`
- `__ Dollars Raised`

### 7. FAQ Framing
- **No Password Authentication**: Zero-login adoption via unique persistent link.
- **Run Logging**: 1-click Strava sync or GPX file upload.
- **Adoption Certificate**: Permanent digital link & print-ready PDF with map trace & metrics.
- **Pass the Torch**: Hand-off mechanism to recruit the next runner in the caretaking chain.
