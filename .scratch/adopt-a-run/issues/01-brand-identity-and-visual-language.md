## Question

What are the core brand guidelines, color palette, typography hierarchy, visual moodboard, and emotional tone for Adopt A Run?

### Focus Areas
- Primary and secondary color palette (moving away from generic primaries to curated, vibrant HSL/gradient tones).
- Typography pairing (heading & body fonts from Google Fonts).
- Brand tone of voice (inspirational, community-focused, energetic, welcoming).
- Logo & icon design direction.
