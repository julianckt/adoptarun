How Adopt A Run Works

A simple 3-step journey connecting fitness, digital art, and social impact.

Step 01 • Discovery

Pick Your Strava Artwork

Browse our curated collection of GPS art routes mapped across Hong Kong's streets and trails — from animals to nature guardians.

HK Community

Run For A Cause

Every artwork is paired with a local charity cause. Take care of the art and champion the mission.

Step 02 • Action

Adopt & Take Care of It

Commit to your run without needing any password or complex account setup. Sync with 1-click Strava or upload your GPX file.

Step 03 • Reward

Get Digital & Print Certificate

Instantly receive a permanent shareable web certificate and high-res printable PDF with your GPS art map trace and runner metrics.
