## 1. Executive Summary

This research analyzes the color landscapes across **sports/running tracking apps** (Strava, Nike Run Club, Garmin Connect, Apple Fitness+, Adidas Running, NRC) and **sports charity/fundraising organizations** (Movember, UNICEF Run, Great Run, St. Jude, TCS NYC Marathon).

The goal is to pinpoint a color palette that **unmistakably signals athletics, energy, and GPS tracking**, while remaining **distinctive, rare, and visually memorable** in a sea of clichés.

---

## 2. Industry Standard & Overused Color Palettes

### 2.1 The "Overused" Spectrum

1. **Strava Orange (`#FC4C02`) + White / Dark Gray**
   * **Where used**: Strava, Runkeeper, TrainingPeaks.
   * **Verdict**: Extremely saturated in the running space. Any brand using bright orange + white immediately looks like a Strava clone.
2. **Nike High-Vis Volt (`#CEFF00`) + Pure Pitch Black (`#000000`)**
   * **Where used**: Nike Running, NRC, Gymshark, high-fashion streetwear.
   * **Verdict**: High energy, but feels aggressive, exclusive, and industrial rather than community-warm or cause-driven.
3. **Garmin / Corporate Blue (`#007ACC` / `#004B87`) + Neutral Gray**
   * **Where used**: Garmin Connect, Polar Beat, Strava corporate features.
   * **Verdict**: Feels clinical, tech-corporate, and sterile. Lacks community excitement and creative artistic expression.
4. **Charity Pink & Red (`#E11D48` / `#EC4899`)**
   * **Where used**: Breast Cancer 5Ks, Red Nose Day, traditional charity runs.
   * **Verdict**: Signals charity, but lacks athletic edge, modern GPS art aesthetics, and urban sport feel.

## 3. What Defines the Athletic & Running Space?

To be recognized **unmistakably as a sports brand**, a palette requires:

- **High Luminance Contrast**: Fast visual processing; sports UI requires readability at a glance.
- **Electric Vector Accents**: Neon/luminous accents that mirror GPS polyline tracks, night running reflectives, and electronic race timing.
- **Deep Grounding Tones**: Charcoal, Slate, or Deep Indigo backgrounds that give luminescence to route maps and metric data badges.

---

## 4. The "Edge" Palette for Adopt A Run

To sit right at the edge — **unmistakably athletic yet distinctively artistic and community-warm** — we combine two contrasting spectrums:

1. **Electric Acid Lime (`#A3E635` / `hsl(84, 81%, 55%)`)**
   * *Role*: Primary Brand & Polyline Vector Accent.
   * *Signal*: Universal runner safety/high-vis equipment color + living, growing caretaking energy. Unlike Nike's harsh Volt, Acid Lime carries an organic, vibrant nature undertone (perfect for animal art & caretaking).
2. **Hyper Deep Violet / Obsidian Slate (`#0F0C1B` / `hsl(255, 38%, 7%)`)**
   * *Role*: Primary Background & Canvas.
   * *Signal*: Replaces generic pitch black or slate blue with a rich, midnight obsidian tone. Creates a gallery-like backdrop for GPS art while feeling sleek and premium.
3. **Electric Solar Cyan / Horizon Teal (`#06B6D4` / `hsl(188, 94%, 43%)`)**
   * *Role*: Secondary Accent & Metric Badge.
   * *Signal*: Signals GPS tracking, altitude, water, and digital precision without feeling corporate blue.
4. **Warm Coral Sunset (`#FF6B52` / `hsl(8, 100%, 66%)`)**
   * *Role*: Call-to-Action & Charity Heart Accent.
   * *Signal*: Human warmth, compassion, and charity engagement — balancing the high-tech athletic neon with genuine emotional heart.

## 6. Why This Palette Stands Out

1. **Avoids the "Strava Clone" Trap**: No `#FC4C02` orange dominance.
2. **Avoids Harsh Nike Industrialism**: Replaces cold black & harsh yellow with an enriched **Obsidian Violet + Acid Lime + Coral Warmth** trinity.
3. **Balances Tech + Heart**: Acid Lime & Cyan provide the athletic/tech precision, while Coral Warmth delivers the non-profit charity soul.
4. **GPS Art Gallery Feel**: Midnight Obsidian background makes any animal Strava art route polyline look like a glowing digital neon masterpiece.
